I

Last season — the team

True level

The table understates Vitesse by eight places. The football flatters them by two or three.

Everyone at the club has done the first half of this arithmetic: at 56 points Vitesse finish 7th on goal difference and displace Roda JC from the play-offs. The second half is that the underlying performance sat in the eighth-to-eleventh band, ninth of the sixteen clubs that can be promoted.

Both corrections are real, and both are needed. The 56 points were close to deserved: both models and the bookmakers put the over-performance between one and four points, which is one result. The gap between 7th and 10th is mediocre underlying performance rather than luck.

Across the division the two xG models’ season totals agree within about 2%; on Vitesse specifically they disagree by 3.5 on xG difference (6.8 on xG for), because one model credits Vitesse’s direct, crossed shot diet (41% of shots from outside the box) more than the other. How they played · 2025/26

Last season: slower, longer, sideways less often, and a mid-block that defended by shape rather than pressure.

Everything in this section describes the completed 2025/26 season, the team Vitesse were. The first five matches of 2026/27 already look different on several of these measures, and that comparison is in Part III. The Twelve Football season report and the licensed Wyscout match data describe the same team the independent model does. The Twelve report ranks Vitesse against the other nineteen on seven phases; the licensed Wyscout export gives the match-level detail behind it. The pattern is consistent: a direct, low-possession side that let opponents have the ball outside the box and rarely inside it, shot at the first sight of goal, and got the league’s second-worst shot quality as a result.

The edge

The one fixable edge is corners, and it is worth about +3.6 expected goals.

Set pieces are a fifth of the expected goals in this division. Vitesse are sixth of twenty at them and first at indirect free kicks, so the club can already coach a dead ball. They are twelfth at corners, and outside the top ten at corners in four of the last six seasons. That is the hole, and it is the part of the game a wage bill grips least.

It persists. Set-piece xG difference carries year to year at r = +0.74 across 160 club-seasons (better than open-play xG difference in this division), and a club retains 77% of its set-piece spread into the following season. A finishing residual retains 33%. The mechanism is aerial: 42% of aerial duels won against the opposition’s 46%.

The size, said in bands rather than gaps: halve the distance to Willem II and Vitesse move from +2.8 to about +6.4 on xG difference. Over six seasons, clubs in the 0-to-+5 band reached the play-offs two times in eleven; clubs in the +5-to-+10 band, seven times in nine. In points, about two and a half. Say it before it is asked, then rank it third behind the recruitment decision and the contract decisions.

Four cases

What the numbers settle, and what they don’t.

Zonneveld: expect fewer goals, and do not read it as decline

+3.8goals above expected

Nine non-penalty goals from 5.17 expected, the second-largest residual in the division. A player’s finishing residual persists at r = +0.045 year to year: 96% of it evaporates. Expect materially fewer goals next season even if he plays exactly as well. One caveat: at 20 he is in the population where a residual is most likely to contain real development. His chance quality (0.32 NPxG per 90) is the real number, and it is the third best at the club.

0.443NPxG per 90 · 15th of 355

He has started all five matches this season; in the first four he scored five, from 3.76 expected: over 1.0 NPxG per 90 on a sample too small to quote as a level and large enough to say the role is his. Büttner: the exception nobody would guess

Ball progression per 90: best at the club, ahead of every midfielder, at 37, from left-back. Switches of play first among the division’s full-backs (MyGamePlan). He is a specialist, the club’s best at getting the ball forward, in a role that has already been reduced this season.

36of 38 matches

3,144 minutes, all but two league matches, and the highest floor in the squad: on a third-party per-match rating (MyGamePlan) he did not have a bad game all season. Ten points from fifteen. Over the four matches the independent model covers, an xG difference of +2.1: results and performance have met in the middle.

Five matches is a sample too small to support any performance claim; it is a reliable guide to selection. Twelve Earpiece scouting reports exist for each of the three. Each rates the player on nine facets against a pool of peers in his league, and each facet on three to seven underlying metrics with a value and a rank. What follows is that data, with three things added: the size of the pool and the sample, which decide what a rank can mean; what the numbers support and what they cannot; and where the report’s own machine-written text contradicts its own table. Two of the three reports rate the player before he joined: Wouters on his 2025/26 season at RKC Waalwijk, Dahbo on his 2026 Allsvenskan minutes at Häcken. Each fit is judged against how Vitesse are playing this season: five matches, ten points, an xG difference of +2.1 over the four the independent model covers, a back four since match 2 and the same seven starters throughout. On the licensed data for the first three matches the side keeps more of the ball (57% against 48% in 2025/26), plays fewer long passes (10% of passes against 14%), crosses more (26 a match against 17) and wins more in the air (57% of aerial duels against 42%). Three matches describe an intent; the fits below are read with that in mind.

The calendar

Fourteen contracts end on 30 June 2027. The club’s hold over them ends on 1 January.

Fourteen of the twenty-seven contracts on Transfermarkt run out on the same day, 30 June 2027. Six of them belong to the settled eleven: Brüll, Zumberi, Olde Keizer, Schwarz, Sellouf and Bannis. Dahbo’s loan ends then too. It is a cliff because they all end at once: more than half the squad and most of the first team have to be decided in one window.

Fixing it costs nothing, which is why it ranks ahead of any signing on this page. In this division fees average about €15,500 and three-quarters of permanent signings are free, so a player’s contract length decides whether he leaves as an asset the club can sell or as an expense who walks out for nothing. Extending, selling or releasing costs a decision and no fee.

The deadline is earlier than the expiry date. A player in the last six months of his contract can sign a pre-contract with another club, so for a 30 June expiry that window opens on 1 January 2027. From that day the club cannot sell him for a fee, because any buyer can wait and take him free in July, and any extension is negotiated against an offer the player may already hold. The real decision point for all fourteen is December.

A second date sits behind it. Under the Dutch standard player contract, a club that misses the 31 March notice deadline can find a player it meant to release renewed for another year by operation of law. That is the same kind of failure as the filing deadlines that cost 57 points.

Sort the fourteen in September, into extend, sell in January, or release, while the club still has the choice.

The head coach’s contract also runs to summer 2027 and resolves in the same window; that is the first continuity decision. A count on the wider registered list gives eighteen of thirty-three. The denominators differ, the argument does not.
